FAQ

Good questions deserve clear answers.

No agency jargon. These are the real questions people ask us before getting started.

Ask us directly
How long does a project take?+
Most websites take 3–6 weeks. A focused landing page can be done in 1–2 weeks. More complex platforms typically run 6–10 weeks. We give you a realistic timeline after our first call — not a guess.
Do you provide hosting?+
We deploy to Vercel, Netlify, AWS, or your preferred provider. Everything stays in your name. We handle the technical setup and can manage it ongoing if needed.
Can you redesign my existing website?+
Yes. We audit what you have, identify what's working, and rebuild with better performance and design — while keeping any SEO value you've already built.
Do you build custom web systems?+
Absolutely. Proposal tools, client portals, booking systems, invoice platforms — we scope and build custom web apps designed for maintainability so you're not dependent on us forever.
Is SEO included?+
Technical SEO is built into every project — semantic HTML, heading hierarchy, meta tags, Open Graph, structured data, and fast loading. Content strategy and ongoing SEO are separate.
What's your pricing like?+
Pricing depends on scope, not a price list. A landing page and a multi-page platform are very different projects. We'll give you a clear number after understanding what you actually need.

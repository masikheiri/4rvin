FAQ
How long does a project take? ▾
Most projects take 4–8 weeks depending on scope. A focused landing page can be done in 2–3 weeks. Complex platforms with custom backends typically run 8–16 weeks. We'll give you a precise timeline after the discovery call.
Do you provide hosting? ▾
We deploy to Vercel, AWS, or your preferred provider. We handle the full deployment pipeline and can optionally manage hosting on your behalf with monthly retainers. All accounts are owned by you.
Can you redesign existing websites? ▾
Absolutely. Redesigns are a core part of our practice. We audit your existing site, identify conversion gaps, and rebuild with improved UX, performance, and visual quality — while preserving SEO equity wherever possible.
Do you build custom systems? ▾
Yes. Custom CMS backends, booking systems, client portals, and API integrations are well within our scope. We architect for maintainability so your team can self-manage after launch.
Do you offer SEO optimization? ▾
Technical SEO is built into every project: semantic HTML, Core Web Vitals optimization, structured data, sitemap generation, and meta strategy. For ongoing content SEO we work with specialist partners.

FAQ

Good questions
deserve clear answers.

No confusing agency speak. These are the real questions people ask us before getting started.

Ask us directly
How long does a project take? +
Most websites take 3–6 weeks. A focused landing page can be done in 1–2 weeks. More complex platforms with custom backends typically run 6–10 weeks. We'll give you a realistic timeline after our first call — not a guess.
Do you provide hosting? +
We deploy to Vercel, Netlify, or your preferred provider. Everything stays in your name and under your control. We handle the technical setup and can manage it ongoing if needed.
Can you redesign my existing website? +
Yes. We'll audit what you have, identify what's working, and rebuild with better performance, cleaner code, and improved design — while keeping any SEO value you've already built.
Do you build custom web systems? +
Absolutely. Proposal tools, client portals, booking systems, invoice platforms — if you need a custom web app for your business, we can scope and build it. We design for maintainability so you're not dependent on us forever.
Is SEO included? +
Technical SEO is baked into every project — semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, meta tags, Open Graph, structured data, and fast loading. Content strategy and ongoing SEO are separate services we can discuss.
What's your pricing like? +
Pricing depends on scope, not on a price list. A clean landing page and a multi-page platform are very different projects. We'll give you a clear, honest number after understanding what you actually need.
